Loot Box Odds Audit

How top-grossing video games show the odds of their loot boxes to players in four countries with different rules.

Results table Explainer for parents and players Code and data

What this is

On one snapshot date, the best-selling games on the iPhone App Store, Steam, PlayStation Store, Xbox Store and Nintendo eShop were collected for South Korea (where showing odds is required by law), the United Kingdom (industry guidelines), the United States (platform rules and FTC enforcement) and Belgium (paid loot boxes treated as gambling). For each game, automated checks looked for a loot box label and for published odds on the store page and the publisher's website.
